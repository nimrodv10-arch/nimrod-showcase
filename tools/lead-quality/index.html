Lead Quality Dashboard

A comparison-site lead-gen business buys search traffic, sends users to partners, and gets paid per approved lead. Cheap leads are not the goal: approved revenue per click is. This view finds where the two disagree.

Synthetic data. 8 B2B software partners × 12 weeks, generated in the browser. The KPI structure mirrors what I tracked as a Growth Manager; no real company numbers are used.

Definitions

Site CTR
Out-clicks to partner / site clicks.
CPL · CPQL
Cost per lead · cost per qualified lead.
Approval rate
Approved leads / leads, as reported back by the partner.
EPC
Approved revenue per paid click. The number bidding should optimise for.
ROAS
Approved revenue / cost (often labelled "ROI" internally).
Prediction gap
Approved revenue vs. revenue predicted when the lead arrived. A widening gap means quality dropped before the partner said so.
