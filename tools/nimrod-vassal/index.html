How I open a new vertical

Prove the money before building the site

Most new-vertical ideas sound good. The job is to find the few worth the product and dev effort, and to stop the rest early.

  1. Demand screen. Market size, hype, and real search volume from Keyword Planner, scored High / Mid / Low.
  2. Partner and deal feasibility. Talk to the companies that would pay us. Who will work with us, on what model (CPL, CPA, RevShare), at what terms.
  3. Build only what passed. One site or a hub of sites, with product, content, and dev.
  4. Plan the launch month backwards from the target.
  5. Measure against the market, watch the red flags, and stop in time.
target qualified leads→site CTR→clicks→CPC→weekly & monthly budget

Timing matters too: we avoided B2B launches in August and B2C launches in May–June, and preferred September–October.
